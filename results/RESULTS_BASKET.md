### Answer to 'is there a single winning strategy?'

**Yes — emphatically.** Across these 9 assets, **89 of 126** strategy/asset combinations were profitable. Trend/breakout systems (Donchian, Keltner, momentum) made money on almost everything.

### Answer to 'does any reach $1,000,000 from $50?'

**No.** Winning and 50,000×-ing are different questions. These strategies *win* (positive expectancy); none *moonshot* a $50 stake to seven figures, because that needs an edge × frequency that doesn't exist on daily bars.

### So how do people actually make money?

- **On real capital, not $50.** 15-30%/yr on $100k is a great living; the same % on $50 is $7.50. The percentages are identical — the strategy isn't the problem, the base is.
- **Compounding over years/decades**, not weeks.
- **Edge + risk control + size**, accepting drawdowns most people can't stomach.
- Many 'traders' make money from **fees, spreads, market-making, or selling courses** — not from turning pocket change into millions.
